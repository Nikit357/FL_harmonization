# What this folder does not contain, and why

This folder is the public material for the major revision of Article 1 (NAR Genomics and
Bioinformatics, NARGAB-2026-312). It is a one-way, sanitized copy of the authors' working folder,
regenerated on every sync. Everything in the working folder is here **except** what is listed
below. No list in this file names a sample.

Fifteen of the 88 cohorts in the benchmark are proprietary or under controlled access (see
`../DATA_AVAILABILITY.md`). Their **names** and their **per-approach and per-batch metrics** appear
throughout `output/` and the figures. Their **samples and expression values** appear nowhere.

## Withheld files

| What | Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Manuscript and response-letter Word files (`Revised_manuscript/**/*.docx`) | 14 | Drafts with tracked changes and comments. The published article is the text of record |
| The editor's decision letter with the reviewer reports, and the draft response that quotes them | 2 | The journal states that reviewer reports are confidential and are not published without the Editors' permission |
| A text dump of the submitted manuscript (`inputs/baseline_dump.txt`) | 1 | Same reason as the Word files |
| Kubernetes pod manifests (`k8s/`) | 2 | Specific to the authors' cluster. `RUNBOOK.md` describes the pod steps with placeholders (`${K8S_NAMESPACE}`, label `app=fl-revision`) |
| Local caches (`cache/`) and editor / OS files | 1 + the git-ignored cache | Regenerated by the scripts; `cache/` holds per-sample matrices |

## Rows removed from per-sample tables

`output/wp6/designs/*.tsv` (the gradient-experiment sample lists) and
`output/wp7/fold_samples.tsv` (the leave-cohort-out folds) list one sample per row. Rows of the
15 proprietary or controlled-access cohorts are removed: **27,522 of 98,908
rows** across these files. The three copies of `Supplementary File 2.xlsx` lose the same samples
from their `Genes_per_sample` sheet; the other sheets are complete.

Consequence: these designs and folds document which open samples each experiment used. A re-run
of `python_scripts/r10_build_gradient_designs.py` or `r20_build_cohort_folds.py` on the open
annotation draws *different* designs, because the published runs drew from all 7,174 samples.

## Text replaced

Author affiliation, funding and competing-interest wording points to the published article.
Cluster namespaces, pod names, storage volumes, the S3 bucket and absolute paths on the authors'
machines are replaced by placeholders (`${K8S_NAMESPACE}`, `fl-revision`, `$FL_S3_BUCKET`,
`$FL_DATA_ROOT`, `~/FL_harmonization`). The internal label of the normal B-cell reference group is
`Normal_B_cells`, as in the rest of this repository. No number, table value, method name or result
was changed.

## What re-runs from this repository

* `python -m pytest tests/ -q`, and the analysis layer (`python_scripts/r01`–`r05`, `r13`,
  `r22`, `r40`, `r45`, `final_notebooks/nb01`–`nb08`) read the published metric tables and
  `output/`.
* The pod layer (`r11`, `r12`, `r19`, `r21`, `r30`) needs the harmonized matrices
  (`FL_S3_BUCKET`), which hold proprietary samples and are not public.
* `r00`, `r50`, `r60`, `r90` and `r99` read the withheld manuscript files.
* `FROZEN_MD5.txt` lists the private MD5 of each frozen benchmark file. Some of those files
  differ here, by the sanitization only, and some are withheld; `FROZEN_MD5_public.txt` gives
  both MD5s and the status of each, so `r99_verify.py`'s MD5 check reports them by design.
