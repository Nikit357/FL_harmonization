## 2. Defects that survived both review rounds

### D1 - Two manuscript sentences quote numbers that have no locus under `tables/` (rule 1 failure)

*Owner: numbers agent. Raised at gate 3 round 1, unchanged at round 2, unchanged at delivery.*

`audit_numbers.py --tables tables/ --evidence 01_numbers.json` exits 1. Three values are involved,
in two sentences:

- `198` - "Of the 198 approaches whose batch PCReg exceeded 0.9999, generated by six methods
  (03_limma, 13_fsmvn, 07_pycombat, 21_harmonizr, 33_amdbnorm and 28_npn), 98.5% returned a kBET
  acceptance rate under 0.1."
- `1,359.6` and `555,004.7` - "20_shambhala returned a median of 1,359.6 and a maximum of 555,004.7".

All three are **correct**. The team lead re-derived them at this gate from
`<repo>/harmonization-metrics/metric_tables/metrics_comprehensive_260905.csv`,
renaming `shambhala_P0std_Q0std` to `20_shambhala` before any filter and inner-joining to the
2,234-row `(strat, imp, method, post_rm)` key of `A2_T1`: `pcr_RNA_BATCH > 0.9999` gives n = 198 from
exactly those six methods, of which 98.5% have `kbet_acceptance_rate_RNA_BATCH < 0.1`; over the 84
`20_shambhala` rows, mean `exp_median` = 1,359.6 and mean `exp_max` = 555,004.7.

What is missing is the provenance chain, not the value: `A2_T1` carries no `pcr_*`, `kbet_*` or
`exp_*` column, and `01_numbers.json` has no entry for either value.

**Required fix (one of two):** carry `pcr_RNA_BATCH`, `kbet_acceptance_rate_RNA_BATCH` and the
`exp_*` columns into a file under `tables/`; or add both values to `01_numbers.json` naming
`metrics_comprehensive_260905.csv`, the column and the filter. **Daniil decides** whether to promote
the columns or to accept the exception knowingly before submission.

### D2 - The same sentence misdescribes its statistic

*Owner: writer. Raised at gate 3 round 2, unchanged.*

maximum". The median of `exp_median` is 1,348.7 and the maximum of `exp_max` is 1,873,593.6, so the
two words name quantities the manuscript does not report. The same sentence already uses the correct
phrasing ("mean maximum expression values") for the two ComBat-seq implementations.

**Required fix:** restate as means over the 84 approaches, matching the ComBat-seq phrasing in the
same sentence; bring the .docx into agreement in the same pass (rule 12, plain edits, no tracked
changes).
